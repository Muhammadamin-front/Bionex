## Direction contract

THESIS: The specialist's explanation, printed as the clinic corridor's numbered health poster; the order form is its first
numbered step. Refuses the category's split hero (headline + red box + badge row + form card).

OWN-WORLD: Offset print in three inks on matte stock: ultramarine field (#2443a8, deep #182f7d) with a visible halftone
screen, one ochre ink (#f0b429), black ink, unprinted paper (#fafaf7). Condensed poster grotesque for headlines (self-hosted,
Cyrillic incl. ў қ ғ ҳ); reading text in the phone's own sans (Roboto / SF), chosen over a self-hosted humanist face to
keep the form ready fast on slow networks (AGENTS.md #62). Panels are paper rectangles framed by thin double printed rules
and numbered ochre roundels; the box is printed as a one-ink AM halftone (ultramarine dots on ochre) and faces as two-ink
duotones (ultramarine-deep × ochre). Pictograms in one stroke, drawn as print.

STORY: Recognise yourself in three plain words (pressure, headache, tiredness) → see that a specialist rings and explains,
nothing to pay now → leave name and number in step 1 → read steps 2–3, what the specialist explains, who uses it (faces,
numbers, certificate) → the poster's footer repeats step 1.

FIRST VIEWPORT (390×844): masthead strip «ЗЕЙНАРА» + drawn phone mark; headline in ochre/white condensed caps over the
halftone field; one-line lead; Step 1 panel (ochre roundel «1», name, +998 phone, ochre «Зейнарага ариза қолдириш») fully
visible by 640 px (button whole at 320×568); steps 2 and 3 as a ruled strip directly under it, entering a 390×844 screen;
the form's pay line and call box are left to the closing form.

FORM: poster with numbered panels, my list position 6 of 7; seed: none (unavailable) — the roll printed no SEED REFERENCE
line, and no check-seed command could confirm one. Signature: the box's halftone print lifting away to the photograph as it comes into view; motion
grammar: print-plate settle, one orchestrated moment.
